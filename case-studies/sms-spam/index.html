SMS Spam Detection

Spam is a major issue in personal privacy and security with respect to technological innovations such as Short Messaging Services (SMS). SMS spam specifically is the act of sending unsolicited messages containing advertising (not harmful) as well as malicious (harmful) links. As a Data Science Manager at Cyber Solutions, I was tasked with using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify and classify those malicious messages before employees receive them on their phones.
