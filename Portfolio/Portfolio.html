5,000 Biggest Cities of the World

An interactive map built from a list of cities. Each city gets a colored dot based on population — purple for big cities, green for smaller ones — and you can click a dot for the city's name and population. A heat layer makes denser areas brighter, you can switch map styles, and a small inset map helps you navigate.

Snake Odyssey

A twist on the classic Snake game. Collect red apples (1 point) and rare green apples (2–5 points). Every 10 apples a portal appears that takes you to a faster level with new obstacles.

How to playHide instructions
  1. Click Start Game in the middle of the board.
  2. Steer with the arrow keys; the snake keeps moving until you turn.
  3. Eat red apples for 1 point and green apples for 2–5 points. Green apples only last 5–10 seconds.
  4. After 10 points a black portal appears. Enter it to reach the next level — 5% faster, with new gray obstacles.
  5. Avoid the walls, obstacles and your own tail.
  6. Your level, score and time show above the board.
