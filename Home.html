About

I started my career at Accenture Federal, supporting the healthcare.gov fix and the Department of Defense's $4.3B electronic health record rollout. After an MBA from Emory's Goizueta Business School, I moved into product — guiding Oracle Health's HealtheCRM and launching Salesforce and generative-AI products at Southern Glazer's Wine & Spirits.

Today I'm a Senior Consultant at Ingage Partners in Cincinnati, building internal products and leading AI enablement. I hold the Salesforce Agentforce Specialist and Microsoft Certified AI Business Professional certifications, and completed MIT's No Code AI and Machine Learning program.
