About me

I'm a product person who lives in the messy middle — the stretch between a good idea and people actually using it. I've spent twelve years there, in very different worlds.

I started at Accenture Federal, working on the healthcare.gov rescue and then spending years on the road helping the Department of Defense roll out its new electronic health record across military hospitals. That's where I learned that technology only matters if the people on the floor adopt it.

After an MBA at Emory's Goizueta Business School — including a semester at the Stockholm School of Economics — I moved into product. I guided Oracle Health's $28M HealtheCRM through heavy team turnover, then built Proof 360 and a first-of-its-kind generative-AI Next Best Action tool at Southern Glazer's Wine & Spirits.

Today I'm a Senior Consultant at Ingage Partners in Cincinnati. I build internal products, lead our AI enablement, research corporate development opportunities for leadership, and designed an AI curriculum for middle schoolers through Bring Your Own Computer.

Outside work, I keep bees, make pottery, grow pollinator gardens, and have followed quantum computing for about a decade. Star Trek: The Next Generation is still my favorite show — Captain Picard is a surprisingly good guide to working with AI.
